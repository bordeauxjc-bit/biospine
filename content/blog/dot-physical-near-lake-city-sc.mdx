## What happens during the appointment

The examination follows FMCSA's Medical Examination Report, Form MCSA-5875. It includes:

- A review of your health history and medications
- Vision and hearing checks
- Blood pressure and pulse
- A urinalysis for specific gravity, protein, blood, and sugar
- A physical examination of the body systems listed on the federal form
- A qualification decision based on the current standards and your findings

The required urinalysis is not the same as an employer drug test. Scheduling the exam also does not guarantee certification; the Medical Examiner may need records or follow-up before making a decision.

## What to bring to a DOT physical

Bring your driver's license or other government photo ID, a complete medication list, and the glasses, contact lenses, or hearing aids you use while driving. If you are being treated for an ongoing condition, relevant records can prevent an avoidable delay.

Some drivers need an FMCSA assessment form completed by a treating clinician before the exam. This may apply to insulin-treated diabetes, an alternative vision standard, or a medication that needs clarification. The [BioSpine DOT paperwork section](/services/dot-physicals#dot-paperwork) links directly to the current federal forms.

Do not stop a prescribed medication before the exam unless the clinician who manages it tells you to.
